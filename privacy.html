4utube Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What the app does

4utube is a personal tool. It runs on the owner's own computer. It uploads video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data the app uses

The app asks Google for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. Google gives the app a sign-in token. The app stores this token on the owner's computer only.

Data sharing

The app does not collect data from other people. The app does not sell, share or send data to any third party. The app sends video files and titles only to YouTube.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Remove access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time on the Google account permissions page.
